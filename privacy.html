Privacy Policy

Ohio State Roleplay Utilities
Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Ohio State Roleplay Utilities ("the Bot") is a Discord bot designed to provide Discord moderation and Emergency Response: Liberty County (ER:LC) private-server utilities.

2. Information We Process

The Bot may process information required to provide its features, including Discord user IDs, Discord server IDs, usernames, Discord roles, command inputs, and moderation-related information.

When ER:LC commands are used, the Bot may send the required command information to the ER:LC API to perform the requested server action.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate the Bot, verify permissions, perform requested Discord or ER:LC actions, respond to commands, and maintain the Bot's functionality.

4. Data Storage

The Bot does not intentionally sell or share personal information for advertising purposes.

Some moderation information may be stored temporarily while the Bot is operating. Certain information, such as warnings stored in memory, may be lost when the Bot restarts.

5. Third-Party Services

The Bot uses third-party services including Discord and the ER:LC API. These services have their own privacy policies and terms of service.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect Bot credentials and configuration. Users should never provide passwords, API keys, Discord bot tokens, or other private credentials through Bot commands.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Bot's features or data handling practices change.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the owner or administrator of the Discord server where the Bot is used.
